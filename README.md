# Self Streak

A personal dashboard for goals, money, bills, debt, reminders, health and grooming, built for life in Lucknow.

**Open it:** download `index.html` and open it in any browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch `main`, folder `/`).

## Install it on your phone
Open https://self-streak.adityaps700.workers.dev on your phone.
- **Android (Chrome):** menu ⋮ → Install app.
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share → Add to Home Screen, then open it from the home screen.

Then go to **Notifications** and tap **Turn on notifications**.

## What's inside
- **Overview**: net worth, income vs spending, monthly budget pace, health score, goals, upcoming payments, a habit cairn that stacks a stone as you go, and a one-line daily journal with mood
- **Goals**: progress bars, milestones, auto-tracking for debt and emergency fund
- **Reminders**: full-year calendar (birthdays, tax dates, renewals, festivals) with a phone-calendar export (.ics)
- **Health**: score dial, 12-glass water tracker, full-day streak, 16-week habit heatmap (tap a day to edit it), striped steps chart, plus daily to yearly habits
- **Grooming**: AM/PM routine, haircut tracker, product shelf with "running low" alerts
- **Animated UI**: page-entry motion, count-up numbers, self-drawing charts, a bin that eats the word "Delete", an expanding search, a circle-reveal theme switch and a spinning-tiles unlock. Turn animations off in Settings if you prefer.
- **Quick log (mic button)**: say "did gym, 10k steps, spent 250 on Swiggy" and it lands in the right places, with Undo. Works in English and Hindi.
- **Bank statement import**: drop a PDF, Excel, CSV or photo of a statement. You review the transactions before anything is saved; salary and self-transfers are skipped, and bill/EMI payments tick off the matching bill.
- **Notifications**: water 5× a day, steps, every habit at its own time, bills/EMIs, birthdays, haircut and running-low products, with quiet hours and a one-tap phone-calendar backup.
- **AI (optional)**: add your own Claude API key in Settings → AI for PDF/photo statements and smarter voice logging. The key stays on your device.
- **Life checklist**: the money, insurance, health and safety basics to set up
- **Money**: activity log, budget vs actual, bills tracker, debt payoff planner, savings, year summary

## Privacy
- Your data is saved only in your own browser (localStorage). It is never uploaded, and it is not part of this repo.
- The dashboard is password-locked. Only a salted, stretched hash is stored in the code, never the password. This lock keeps out casual snooping on a shared device; it is not encryption.
- You can change the password for your browser in **Settings**.
- Use **Settings → Export backup** regularly. Clearing browser data erases the dashboard's data.
