Refunds & cancellation

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Self Streak Plus is a monthly digital subscription. We want you to pay only if it's useful, which is why every account starts with a 7-day free trial.

Two ways to pay

  • Autopay (monthly): you approve a ₹499/month mandate once with UPI Autopay or a card through Razorpay. It renews every month until you turn it off.
  • Pay once: a single ₹499 payment through Cashfree for 30 days. It doesn't renew.

Cancellation

  • Autopay: turn it off any time in Settings → Account → Turn off autopay, or cancel the mandate in your UPI app or with your card issuer. You keep Plus until the end of the month you've already paid for, and nothing more is charged.
  • If you cancel before your first autopay charge, you aren't charged at all. Any small amount your bank holds to verify the mandate (usually ₹1–5) is returned by your bank.
  • Pay once: there's nothing to cancel. Your access simply ends when the 30 days end.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings. Deleting your account also stops autopay.

Refunds

  • Charged by mistake or charged twice: we refund the extra payment in full.
  • Paid but couldn't use the service (for example, Plus wasn't activated or the app didn't work for you): email us within 7 days of the payment for a full refund.
  • Forgot to cancel autopay: email us within 7 days of the renewal charge and we'll refund it if you haven't used Plus since.
  • After 7 days, payments for a period that has already started aren't refundable, except where required by law.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are made to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) through Razorpay or Cashfree (whichever you paid with) within 5–7 working days of approval. Your bank may take a few more days to show it.

Delivery

Self Streak is delivered online. Access to Plus starts immediately after a successful payment. There is no physical shipping.

Request a refund

Email us with your account email and the payment date or order ID (shown in your payment receipt).
