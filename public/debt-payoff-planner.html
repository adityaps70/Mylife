Avalanche or snowball?

Avalanche pays extra on the highest-interest debt first (usually credit cards at 36–45% a year), which saves the most money. Snowball clears the smallest balance first, which gives quick wins and keeps you motivated. Self Streak shows both and how many months and how much interest each saves.

Questions

Is the AI plan financial advice?

No. It’s a planning tool based on the numbers you enter. For big decisions, talk to your lender or a registered adviser.

Can it handle credit cards with minimum due?

Yes. Cards and pay-later accounts are tracked with statement dates, minimum due and interest, and included in the plan.

What if my EMIs are more than my income?

The plan will tell you plainly and suggest options like restructuring, extending tenure or consolidating high-interest debt.
