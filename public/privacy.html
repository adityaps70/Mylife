Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what Self Streak collects, why, and the choices you have. We collect only what's needed to run the app.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Your content: the habits, health logs, notes, budgets, bills, loans, cards, reminders and other entries you add. It's stored so it can sync across your devices.
  • Payments: your email (and mobile number for one-time payments) are passed to Razorpay or Cashfree to process a payment. We keep the order or subscription ID, amount and status, never card, UPI or bank details.
  • Push notifications: if you allow notifications, your browser gives us a push address so we can send your reminders.
  • Usage counts: the number of AI actions you use each day, to apply the daily limit.

How we use it

  • To run the app, sync your data and send the reminders you set up.
  • To process payments and confirm your subscription.
  • To contact you about your account or payments.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or share it for marketing.

Service providers

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and stores your data.
  • Anthropic processes text, voice transcripts, photos and PDFs you send to AI features, only to return the result.
  • Razorpay (monthly autopay) and Cashfree Payments (one-time payments) process payments.
  • Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which may be provided by your browser maker (for example Google or Apple).

Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored with salted PBKDF2 hashing. Sessions are kept in secure, HTTP-only cookies.

Your choices

  • Download a copy of your data from Settings → Export backup.
  • Delete your account from Settings. This permanently removes your account and data from our servers.
  • Turn notifications off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Children

Self Streak isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we'll tell you in the app.
