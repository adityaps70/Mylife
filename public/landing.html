FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Self Streak runs in your browser. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen; on Android, tap Install. It then opens like a normal app and can send you notifications.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

You keep all your data. To keep using the app, pay ₹499 for the next month. Nothing is charged automatically; you choose when to pay.

Is my data private?

Your data is stored in your own account and is never sold or shared for advertising. Photos you scan are sent to our AI provider only to read them. You can download or delete everything from Settings at any time.

Does it connect to my bank?

No. Self Streak never asks for bank logins. You can upload a statement PDF or photo, and it reads the payments from it.

Can I cancel?

There's nothing to cancel: each payment covers 30 days and doesn't renew on its own. See our refund policy for refunds.
