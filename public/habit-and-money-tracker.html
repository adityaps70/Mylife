Why track habits and money together

Spending is a set of habits: the daily coffee, the late-night order, the card bill paid in full or only the minimum. A habit tracker alone can’t see any of it, and an expense tracker alone never reminds you to change it. Putting both in one app means the same streak that keeps you going to the gym also keeps you logging spends and sticking to a budget.

It also means one app to open each day instead of two, which is usually the difference between a tracker you keep using and one you forget.

Money habits worth tracking

Any of these can be a habit in Self Streak, with its own reminder and streak:

  • No-spend day (daily or a few days a week): tick it on days you buy nothing beyond essentials.
  • Log every spend before bed (daily): a 30-second habit that keeps the month’s numbers honest, including cash.
  • Review the budget (weekly, say Sunday evening): check which category is running over while there’s still time to fix it.
  • Move savings on payday (monthly): pay yourself first, before the month’s spending starts.
  • Pay the card bill in full (monthly): the bill reminder tells you the amount and the due date.
  • Check subscriptions (quarterly): cancel the OTT or app plans you no longer use.
