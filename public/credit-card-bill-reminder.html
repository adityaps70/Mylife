A credit card bill reminder that understands billing cycles

Self Streak tracks each card like your bank does: spends before the statement date go on this bill, spends after it roll to the next one. You see the amount due, the minimum due and your available limit, and you get a reminder before every due date.

Why a normal reminder isn’t enough

A calendar alert tells you a date. It doesn’t tell you how much is due, what the minimum is, or what you’ve already spent on the next bill. That’s how people end up paying only the minimum and carrying interest of around 3–3.75% a month, or missing a due date because two cards are due in the same week.

Self Streak keeps the numbers next to the date, so the reminder actually helps you decide what to pay.

How it works

  1. Add a card with its limit, statement day, due day and minimum %.
  2. Log spends as you go, by typing or saying “spent 1,200 on Amazon on my HDFC card”.
  3. Get reminded before each due date, and pay full or minimum in one tap.

Questions

Is my card number needed?

No. Self Streak never asks for card numbers, CVV or bank logins. You only enter the limit, statement day and due day.

Does it work with pay-later and postpaid accounts?

Yes. Set the minimum due to 100% and it tracks them as “pay the full bill every month”.

Will I get a notification on my phone?

Yes. Install Self Streak on your home screen and turn on notifications to get push reminders, even when the app is closed.
