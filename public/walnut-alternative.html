A Walnut alternative that doesn’t read your SMS

Walnut, the expense tracker many people in India started with, is now called axio. It still tracks spending automatically from your transactional SMS, and it now also offers credit products. Self Streak takes the other route: you log by voice, photo or bank statement, and it never asks for SMS access.

When Walnut (now axio) is the better choice

  • You want spends to appear with zero typing, and you’re fine with SMS access.
  • You also want credit or FD products from the same company.
  • You only want an expense tracker, nothing else.

When Self Streak is the better choice

  • You don’t want any app reading your SMS.
  • You pay in cash too, and want to log it in two seconds by voice.
  • You want bills, EMIs and card cycles tracked with reminders.
  • You want habits and health next to your money.

Questions

Is Walnut still available?

Walnut was renamed axio. As of 13 November 2026 the axio app is still updated on Android, according to APKMirror release listings.

Can Self Streak import my old data?

You can upload bank statements (PDF, Excel, CSV or a photo) and Self Streak reads the payments. You review them before anything is saved.

Is logging by hand slow?

Saying “did gym, spent 250 on Swiggy” logs both at once. Statement import catches anything you missed.
