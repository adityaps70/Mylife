Track habits, spending, bills, EMIs and reminders in one app. Log a spend by voice, scan bills with the camera, and get reminders on your phone.

Download the app (APK)

Free to download. Sign in with Google, Apple or email.

How to install

  1. Tap Download the app above. If Chrome asks, tap Download anyway.
  2. Open the file from the download message, or from Files → Downloads.
  3. If your phone asks, allow Install unknown apps for Chrome (or your browser), then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Self Streak and sign in.

Already have it? Download again and tap Update: your data stays. The app also updates itself with new features each time it opens.

iPhone? Use Self Streak in Safari at selfstreak.com/app and choose Share → Add to Home Screen.
