Two-wheeler loan EMI calculator

A two-wheeler loan is small and short, usually 1 to 4 years. Because the amounts are small, the processing fee and any “low EMI” offers can matter as much as the interest rate.

For a ₹1 lakh two-wheeler loan at 12% a year for 3 years, the EMI is about ₹3,321 a month and the total interest is about ₹19,572. Paying ₹500 extra every month would close it 5 months early and save about ₹3,053 in interest.

What a small prepayment does

Paying ₹500 extra every month on ₹1 lakh at 12% closes the loan in 31 months instead of 36, and total interest falls from ₹19,572 to ₹16,518: a saving of about ₹3,053.

Two-wheeler loans are usually fixed-rate, and some lenders charge a foreclosure fee. On a short loan the interest saved by prepaying is modest, so check that the fee does not cancel it out.

Tax on a two-wheeler loan

There is no tax deduction for a two-wheeler used personally. If it is used for business, interest may be claimed as a business expense.

Tips before you borrow

  • “Zero down payment” offers usually mean a higher loan amount and more interest.
  • Compare the effective rate: a flat rate of 7% can be close to 13% on a reducing balance.
  • Keep the EMI under about 5% of your take-home pay.

How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. All tables on this page use this reducing-balance formula.
