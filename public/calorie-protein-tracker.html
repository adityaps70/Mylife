Questions

How accurate is the photo calorie estimate?

It’s an estimate based on the dish and a typical portion. You can edit the numbers before logging. Describing quantities (“2 rotis, 1 katori dal”) makes it more accurate.

Does it track micronutrients?

The daily summary points out nutrients your meals were likely low in, such as fibre, calcium, iron or magnesium, based on what you ate. It’s guidance, not a lab test.
