How EMI is calculated

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (yearly rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months. In the early months most of the EMI goes to interest; later, more of it repays the principal.

That’s why even a small extra payment each month makes a big difference. It reduces the principal directly, so every following month charges less interest.

Does prepaying a loan always save money?

Prepaying reduces interest, but some fixed-rate loans charge a prepayment fee. As per RBI rules, floating-rate loans to individuals have no foreclosure or prepayment charges. Check your loan agreement.

Should I prepay my loan or invest?

A common rule: clear high-interest debt (credit cards, personal loans) first, and keep an emergency fund. Self Streak’s AI plan weighs this against your own numbers.
