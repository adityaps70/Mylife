Why one app for both

Health and money goals fail the same way: you track hard for a week, then stop opening the app. Keeping both in one place means one habit to keep, opening Self Streak each day, instead of two. Each day’s quests mix them (drink water, log a meal, log an expense, tick your habits), and the evening summary looks at the whole day.

They also affect each other. Cooking at home is both cheaper and usually better for your protein target, and a weekly grocery budget is easier to keep when meals are planned. Seeing food spending and what you ate on the same screen makes that link obvious.

A daily check-in that takes two minutes

  1. Morning: tick your habits as you do them; water reminders run through the day.
  2. Meals: snap a photo, or type “2 rotis, dal and paneer sabzi”.
  3. Spends: say them as they happen, or import the bank statement once a month.
  4. Evening: read the day summary of what went well and what you missed.

Self Streak never asks for bank logins, card numbers or SMS access. Your data is never sold or used for ads, and you can export or delete it any time.

Yes. Voice and text logging understand English, Hindi and Hinglish.
