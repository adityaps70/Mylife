Axis Bank credit card statement date and due date

How the billing cycle, payment due date and interest-free period work on Axis Bank credit cards, taken from the bank’s own most important terms and conditions (MITC).

Short answer: On Axis Bank cards the interest-free period is 20 to 50 days, depending on the date of the transaction. Pay the total amount due by the due date on your statement and you pay no interest. Your exact dates are printed on each statement.

Checked against Axis Bank’s published terms on 13 November 2026. Terms differ by card; your card’s MITC wins. Updated 2026-11-13.

What Axis Bank’s terms say

  • The interest-free period ranges from 20 to 50 days depending on the transaction date. It is suspended for the current statement if any balance from the previous bill was not paid by the due date.
  • Billing cycle dates are the 1st, 7th, 10th, 12th, 13th, 15th, 18th, 20th, 22nd or 25th of the month, and the payment due date falls 20 days after the billing date.
  • You have a one-time option to change your statement date by calling Axis Phone Banking.
  • A late payment fee applies if nothing, or less than the minimum amount due, is received by the due date. A grace period of 3 days from the due date is allowed. If you pay by auto-debit and the due date is a Sunday, the debit happens on the next working day.
  • Cash withdrawals always carry interest from the transaction date.

What this means for you

  • Make big planned purchases just after your statement date to get the longest time to pay.
  • Pay a few working days before the due date, so the money reaches the card account in time.
  • Paying only the minimum keeps the account in good standing but costs a lot of interest; see the credit card interest calculator.
  • For how the dates work on any card, read statement date vs due date (हिंदी में).

Questions

What is the interest-free period on a Axis Bank credit card?

20 to 50 days, depending on the date of the transaction, according to Axis Bank’s own terms, as long as you pay the full previous bill and don’t withdraw cash.

Can I change my Axis Bank card statement date?

You have a one-time option to change your statement date by calling Axis Phone Banking.

Do I need my card number to track this in Self Streak?

No. Add the card’s limit, statement day and due day, and Self Streak tracks this bill and next bill and reminds you before each due date.
