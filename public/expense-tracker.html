Questions

Do I need to connect my bank account?

No. Self Streak never asks for bank logins. You can upload a statement PDF or photo instead, and it reads the payments.

Does it work with UPI payments?

Yes. Log UPI spends by voice or text, or import them from your bank statement.

Is my financial data private?

Your data is stored in your own account, never sold or used for ads, and you can export or delete it any time.
