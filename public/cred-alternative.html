A CRED alternative for tracking bills, not just paying them

CRED is built around paying credit card bills and earning rewards for it. Self Streak is built around knowing what you owe before the bill arrives: each card’s billing cycle, every EMI, your budget and your habits. Many people use both.

When CRED is the better choice

  • You want to pay all your card bills from one app.
  • You want rewards and cashback offers for paying on time.
  • You only need card payments, not budgets, EMIs or habits.

When Self Streak is the better choice

  • You want to see what’s on this bill and the next one while you’re still spending.
  • You have EMIs or loans and want one debt-free date and a payoff plan.
  • You’d rather not add card details or link a bank just to track.
  • You want money, bills, habits and health in one app.

Questions

Is Self Streak a replacement for CRED?

Not exactly. CRED pays bills; Self Streak tracks them. Many people keep paying through their bank, UPI or CRED and use Self Streak to know the amount, the minimum due and the date in advance.

Does Self Streak need my card number?

No. You add a card with its limit, statement day, due day and minimum %. No card number, CVV or bank login.

Can I track pay-later accounts too?

Yes. Set the minimum due to 100% and Self Streak treats it as “pay the full bill every month”.
