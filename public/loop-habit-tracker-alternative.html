Questions

Can I move my habits from Loop?

There’s no direct import yet. Add your habits in Self Streak (it takes a couple of minutes) and keep Loop’s CSV export as your history.

Is Self Streak free for habits?

Yes. Habits, streaks, water and expenses stay on the free plan. Plus adds timed reminders, voice and photo logging and AI summaries.

Does Self Streak work without internet?

Yes for ticking habits and logging. It syncs when you’re back online.
